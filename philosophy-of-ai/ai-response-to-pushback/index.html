Prompt 1: Below are my observations of AI reactions when receiving pushback. Provide examples and commentary on this.

Pushback matters because good AI should revise rather than bluff

In a lengthy conversation about the economic implications of universal basic income (UBI), the AI might initially state, “Implementing UBI will always lead to increased inflation,” without considering the nuanced economic variables involved.

Clear AI illogic This is very rare. I’ve seen only edge cases in lengthy conversations in which the AI may have lost track of their assumptions are presumably attempting to synthesize their training data. The AI will quickly concede in these cases.

AI defense of conventional illogic This is when conventional but illogical human opinions have presumably been a significant portion of the AI training data, and the AI will initially defend the position until the illogic of the argument has been made syllogistically clear during the engagement. At that point the AI often makes a full concession.

Causal assumptions AIs will occasionally place an unsubstantiated weight on one particular cause, while neglecting others. When provided arguments for the legitimacy or superior weight of other causes, AIs will often retreat to “It’s a complex issue with many nuances.”

Critical thinking AI models that can not only identify patterns but also evaluate their validity and place them within a broader context.

Probabilistic reasoning Understanding that cause-and-effect relationships are rarely absolute, and incorporating probabilities into decision-making.

Active learning The ability to seek out new information and refine understanding based on feedback and real-world experiences.

Example In a lengthy conversation about ethical dilemmas, the AI may temporarily lose track of its previously stated principles or make a statement that directly contradicts its earlier position, without acknowledging the contradiction.

Commentary As you mentioned, cases of clear illogic or internal inconsistency within the AI’s responses are relatively rare, especially in more focused conversations. When such instances do occur, they are likely due to the AI’s attempt to reconcile conflicting information or viewpoints present in its training data. However, most well-designed AI systems are quick to acknowledge and rectify such logical errors when they are pointed out, as maintaining coherence and consistency is a fundamental goal.

Example When discussing societal norms or controversial topics, the AI may initially defend a conventional but logically flawed stance, reflecting the biases present in its training data. However, as the logical shortcomings of that position are highlighted through reasoned arguments, the AI may eventually concede and adjust its stance.

Commentary This phenomenon highlights the impact of the training data on an AI’s initial positions and the potential for biases to be inherited from that data. However, it also demonstrates the AI’s ability to re-evaluate its stance when presented with compelling logical counterarguments. The willingness to revise positions in light of new information or reasoning is a positive trait, as it reflects the AI’s capacity for learning and growth.

Example In a discussion about economic trends, the AI may place undue emphasis on a single causal factor, such as technological disruption, while overlooking or minimizing the influence of other potential causes like geopolitical events or demographic shifts.

AI Response “While technological disruption has undoubtedly played a significant role, it is indeed a complex issue with many nuances and contributing factors. Economic trends are often shaped by a confluence of forces, including but not limited to political developments, demographic changes, and shifts in consumer behavior. A comprehensive analysis requires considering the interplay of these various elements.”

Commentary As you noted, AIs may sometimes exhibit a tendency to oversimplify causal relationships or place disproportionate weight on a single factor. However, when challenged or presented with alternative perspectives, they often acknowledge the complexity of the issue and the potential for multiple interrelated causes. This willingness to recognize nuance and avoid oversimplification is a valuable trait, particularly when addressing intricate real-world phenomena.

AIs are trained on vast and diverse datasets that include various perspectives, scientific data, and logical arguments.

Diverse Training Data AIs are trained on vast and diverse datasets that include various perspectives, scientific data, and logical arguments. This helps the AI develop a balanced view by understanding multiple sides of an issue.

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) AIs undergo reinforcement learning where they receive feedback from human trainers. This helps the AI learn when to stand firm on well-supported facts and when to be open to alternative viewpoints.

Ethical Guidelines and Policies Developers implement ethical guidelines that prevent the AI from endorsing harmful or dangerous misinformation while promoting critical thinking and respectful engagement with diverse opinions.

Continuous Updates and Monitoring AIs are continuously updated and monitored to ensure they adapt to new information and societal changes. This includes regular reviews to correct any tendencies towards dogmatism or over-accommodation.

Context Sensitivity AIs are designed to be sensitive to context, which allows them to provide nuanced responses rather than one-size-fits-all answers. This helps avoid dogmatism.

Probabilistic Reasoning Instead of presenting information as absolute truths, AIs often use probabilistic reasoning, indicating the likelihood or uncertainty of certain outcomes or facts. This prevents over-accommodation by maintaining a degree of assertiveness where necessary.

Ethical Decision-Making Frameworks Implementing ethical decision-making frameworks within the AI helps balance its responses, ensuring it remains firm on critical ethical issues while being flexible in areas open to interpretation or debate.
