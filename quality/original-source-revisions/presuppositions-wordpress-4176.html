  2. How can we balance the need for confidence in our decisions with the limitations of inductive reasoning?
  3. Do you think there are any areas of human knowledge where absolute certainty might be achievable? Why or why not?
  4. How can a critical evaluation of our own biases help us overcome the limitations of subjective thinking?
  5. Should scientific theories ever be considered absolutely certain, given the ever-evolving nature of scientific discovery?
  6. In what ways can the concept of “asymptotic approach” be applied to other areas of life besides knowledge acquisition?
  7. Is there any value in holding onto beliefs that have a very strong inductive foundation, even if absolute certainty is impossible?
  8. How can we differentiate between healthy skepticism and a paralyzing inability to make decisions due to uncertainty?
  9. Do you think the concept of intuition can be reconciled with the limitations of inductive reasoning? Why or why not?
  10. What role do emotions play in shaping our confidence in beliefs derived from inductive experience?
  11. Should the concept of “presuppositions” be completely abandoned in the pursuit of knowledge, or can they serve a useful purpose?
  12. Can you think of any historical examples where unquestioning faith in axioms or self-evident truths led to negative consequences?
  13. How can we foster a culture of open-mindedness and adaptability when dealing with knowledge that is constantly evolving?
  14. Does the concept of absolute certainty have any practical applications, even if it’s unattainable for most types of knowledge?
  15. In your opinion, what are the most important things to keep in mind when evaluating the relative certainty of different types of claims?
